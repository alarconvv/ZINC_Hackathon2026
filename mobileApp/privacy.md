# Privacy policy

Draft — pending legal review.

Version 2026-10-beta. ZINC is a general wellness beta. The operator, contact address, hosting arrangements and provider agreements must be completed before public release. This draft makes no claim of compliance with any law.

## What is collected and why

Your account contains your first name, email and a password hash. Your saved information includes age, activity level, limitations, screening answers, clinician sign-off choices, chosen days, weekly plans, sets, effort, pain, yoga feedback, puzzle answers, self-reported relaxation minutes, home-test records and coach conversations. Consent records and plan-change audit events are also saved. These support sign-in, your plan, safety gates, progress, user-approved goals and your export.

Information comes directly from what you enter and from your actions in ZINC. The rules engine derives your tier, schedule, completion summaries and proposals from that information. ZINC does not connect to device health platforms or collect precise location.

## Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

This separate section addresses the health-related information used in this beta. It is a draft for professional review, not a statement that legal requirements have been satisfied.

Health-data categories include your age and activity profile, limitations, screening and sign-off answers, exercise prescriptions and schedules, pain and effort, activity and home-test records, coach messages you choose to send, and derived plans, goals and progress. Sources are you, your recorded actions and the rules engine. Purposes are to prepare your weekly activities, apply the existing safety gates, record your work and respond to coach requests.

ZINC does not sell health data, share it with advertisers, disclose it to other users, or use third-party analytics or health-data crash reporting. There are no configured affiliate recipients in this local build. Future operators must identify any hosting or other processors and their contracts before launch.

Optional AI coach requests send your message and relevant profile, screening result, plan and activity feedback to Google's Gemini API so it can answer your request. This is provider processing; it is not advertising. Use “Adjust without AI” to avoid an AI request. Your password and access token are not sent to Gemini. Do not add information you do not want the provider to receive. Provider retention and data-use settings require review before public release; this draft does not promise provider-side deletion.

You can access and download your stored information through Settings → Download my data. Settings → Delete my account permanently deletes your account, saved state, consent and audit records from the application's active database. You can withdraw from further collection by deleting your account and stopping use. A contact and appeal process must be filled in by the operator: [CONTACT ADDRESS REQUIRED BEFORE PUBLIC RELEASE].

## Storage and retention

The local beta keeps your account and engine state in SQLite until you delete your account. There is no automatic account expiry or retention job. Deletion removes active database rows; database pages, disk copies or future hosting backups may retain residual information until their own retention periods expire. Secure erasure, backup retention, operator access controls and provider terms are unresolved launch requirements.

Native access tokens use the device secure store. The web version uses localStorage. Reminder preferences are stored on the device and local notifications are handled by the operating system. Device text-size and high-contrast overrides currently apply to the running app. Server application logs do not intentionally include health fields, passwords or coach text. Hosting access logs must be reviewed separately before release.
