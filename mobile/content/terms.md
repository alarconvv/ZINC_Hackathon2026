## Purpose and review status

ZINC offers general wellness activities: strength and balance, yoga, reasoning and relaxation. Content is pending professional review. This build has had no clinical or legal review. Follow qualified clinician guidance for your situation. The shortened screening questions are not the full PAR-Q+.

## Before and during activity

Enter accurate information if you choose to use the beta. Follow the displayed version, cue and prescription. Obtain clinician sign-off when the safety gate requires it. Stop if you feel unwell. Stop locks activity and coaching for that saved account state; signing out does not clear the stop. Seek urgent help as directed by the Stop message. Do not use ZINC as an emergency service.

## Your decisions

The server's existing rules engine determines plans and prescriptions. AI coach text is informational and may be unavailable. A proposed goal is not applied until you explicitly accept it. Accepted goals are staged for next week. You can decline and keep your plan. Home tests and relaxation records are self-reported; home-test results do not change prescriptions.

## Accounts and data

Use a password of at least ten characters and do not share your account. The account owner is responsible for activity on their account. Download your data or permanently delete your account in Settings. Read the draft Privacy policy and Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy before consenting to stored health-related information or using the optional AI coach.

## Beta limits

Availability, continuity, backups and recovery are not guaranteed in this build. SQLite, the hosting setup, notification delivery and provider processing need further operational review. Local reminders depend on device permissions and settings. They are not safety alarms. Exercise images are placeholders; follow the written activity version.
