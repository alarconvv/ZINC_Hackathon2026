# ZINC illustration brief — human art handoff

The current strength SVGs are schematic placeholders. Replace them with final artwork using the same basename. Cards prefer PNG, then WebP, then SVG; absent artwork uses a movement icon. Images illustrate a reference movement; the written version and safety cues remain authoritative.

## Shared style
Flat illustration; one adult figure aged about 50; neutral clothing; side or three-quarter view. Sea-blue #126bc5 and ink #12365b on transparent or #e8f3ff background. Square 1024×1024 PNG or SVG (WebP also supported). Keep the same line weight across all 12 images. No text in the image.
