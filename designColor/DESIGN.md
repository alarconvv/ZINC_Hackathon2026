# ZINC aquatic interface

The interface retains ZINC's ocean-blue identity and the user's aquatic references. Deep blue backgrounds and white headings frame pale, rounded data surfaces; a translucent bottom navigation dock stays separate from the chat composer and reserves content space. Manrope headings and DM Sans body copy use visible dark-and-white focus rings.

Progress is an operational weekly board. Its date rail shows the selected scheduled week. The circular gauge is the percentage of scheduled activities completed, not a health or fitness score. The area curve counts completed activities by scheduled day; its points and accessible description contain actual counts. Partial and skipped activities never count as completed. Detailed feedback retains date, activity, movement and set/pose context instead of connecting unlike observations. Reasoning accuracy includes its answer count; breathing minutes are explicitly self-reported. Earlier weeks retain their recorded results.

Use sea-blue #126bc5, deep ocean #063774, pale surface #f4faff, ink #12365b, muted ink #526b84 and rule #d6e7f5. Large rounded data panels and the gauge follow the pinned reference. No animated chart or synthetic health claim is introduced. Keep all safety stops and explicit proposal approvals.
